WHERE CREATIVITY RUNS WILD

Welcome to Jungle Lords! We're thrilled to invite you to our creative compound nestled in the heart of Chicago's west side, encompassing neighborhoods like West Town, Ukrainian Village, Garfield Park, and Humboldt Park. With six years of operation in the Windy City, our journey began in 2019 in Pilsen, where we've had the privilege of collaborating with a myriad of talented individuals.

Since our start, we've continuously evolved, upgrading our equipment and expanding our facility by several thousand square feet. Beyond being a recording studio, Jungle Lords is a sanctuary—a home away from home. Our aim is to craft an unforgettable experience every time you step into our space.

Having worked with renowned names in entertainment, we're equally passionate about nurturing emerging talent. From recording techniques to mastering the intricacies of session flow, we offer young creatives the opportunity to immerse themselves in every facet of the industry.

Community engagement is at the core of our ethos. We pride ourselves on our involvement within the community, striving to uplift and empower fellow creatives. As we look towards the future, our commitment remains —we'll continue to champion talented individuals and push the boundaries of our work.

We're brimming with excitement for the projects that lie ahead and the endless possibilities of tomorrow. Rest assured, we'll spare no effort to ensure every client leaves with nothing short of 100% satisfaction. Begin your creative journey with us—book your private session today. Welcome to Jungle Lords, where creativity knows no bounds. Where creativity runs wild!

CLUB ROOM

Step into Jungle Lords Studio's Club Room, where luxury meets technical excellence. Every detail is meticulously crafted to elevate your creative process, offering a premium selection of state-of-the-art equipment. From the legendary Avalon Design VT-737SP Channel Strip to the Solid State Logic Fusion Analog Master Processor, experience the pinnacle of audio engineering.

Immerse yourself in pristine audio reproduction with our meticulously curated monitoring setup featuring Eve SC207 2-way monitors, Yamaha HS8 speakers, and the powerful Focal Sub12 13-inch Powered Studio Subwoofer. Navigate your projects with unparalleled precision using cutting-edge interfaces like the Apollo x6 and the timeless warmth of the Neve Lunchbox, fully loaded with premium modules.

Capture every nuance of your sound with legendary microphones like the Neumann U87 AI and Neumann TLM102. Whether you're a seasoned professional or a burgeoning talent, Jungle Lords' Club Room sets the stage for unparalleled sonic exploration and creative mastery.
